QUARTERLY INFRASTRUCTURE REVIEW
AnyCompany Logistics — Platform Engineering
Period ending 2025-09-30

SUMMARY

Batch throughput for the parcel-routing workload increased 34 percent over the
quarter, from 41,000 to 55,000 parcels processed per day. The increase came from
the queue-partitioning change deployed on 2025-08-12, not from additional
capacity; average compute spend fell 6 percent over the same period.

FINDINGS

1. Queue partitioning by destination region removed the head-of-line blocking
   that had dominated wait times during the evening peak. Median queue wait fell
   from 11 minutes to 90 seconds.

2. Three of the eight worker pools remain over-provisioned. Pools 4, 6, and 7
   ran below 30 percent utilization for the entire quarter. Alex Rivera
   has been asked to model consolidation into two pools.

3. The retry storm on 2025-09-04 was traced to an unbounded retry loop in the
   address-normalization service. A retry cap shipped on 2025-09-06. No
   recurrence since.

4. Observability coverage is incomplete. The routing service emits no per-parcel
   timing data, so the 34 percent improvement was measured at the queue rather
   than end to end. AnySoftware Monitoring, our monitoring vendor, has quoted
   for instrumentation work.

ACTIONS

- Alex Rivera to deliver the pool-consolidation model by 2025-10-17.
- Platform Engineering to decide on the AnySoftware Monitoring quote by
  2025-10-24.
- Add end-to-end parcel timing before the next review period.
